Admissions Information

The Department of Game Contents & Animation is a full-time undergraduate program in the School of Global Arts, College of Arts. Undergraduate admission for Korean applicants is administered by the Office of Admissions, and for international applicants by the Office of International Affairs. After graduation, students can continue to the Department of Global Creative Arts and the Department of Applied Art and Technology at the Graduate School.

Selection methods, schedules, and required documents are confirmed each year in the admission guidelines. Before applying, be sure to check the original guidelines for the relevant year, and contact the Department office with any questions about the Department.

FAQFAQ

  • Q. What will I learn?What will I learn?

    You study games, animation, and interactive media as a single creative field. In the first year, you complete the School's common curriculum, including ideation and expression, directing, and storytelling. From the second year, you learn 2D/3D graphics, animation and rigging, cinematic directing, game graphics, and game engines. In the third and fourth years, you create works in project courses such as Previsualization, Affective Game Computing, and Prototyping, and complete a graduation project. Real-time engines and AI tools are part of the coursework throughout.

  • Q. Do I have to choose between games and animation when I enter?Do I have to choose game or animation at admission?

    No. You enter a single department, build your foundations through the School's common curriculum in the first year, and from the second year decide for yourself how to balance games and animation through your course choices. Many students do both.

  • Q. Are there opportunities for international exchange?Are there international exchange opportunities?

    Yes. Students can go on exchange to the Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM) in Germany and the Vilnius Academy of Arts (VDA) in Lithuania, and KHM exchange students are selected every semester. Students can also join the Overseas Exchange Development Project, in which they team up with students from art universities abroad to create works. The Department is an official member of an EU Erasmus+ international consortium.

  • Q. Is a practical exam or portfolio required?Is there a practical exam or portfolio?

    For undergraduate admission, it depends on the track. Arts practical-exam tracks include a practical exam, while tracks based mainly on school records do not. Subjects, schedules, and scoring follow the Office of Admissions guidelines for the relevant year.

  • Q. Are classes taught in Korean?Are the lectures in Korean?

    Classes are taught primarily in Korean, with some courses offered in English. International students must meet the Korean language proficiency requirement; those who do not yet meet it can prepare through the Pre-Sessional Course before admission. Please check the Office of International Affairs admission guidelines.

  • Q. Which semester can I enter?Which semester can I enter?

    Korean applicants enter in the spring semester (March). International applicants may apply for either the spring or the fall semester (September). The Graduate School admits students for the spring (March) and fall (September) terms; applications for the spring 2027 term are accepted from October 16 to 26, 2026.

  • Q. What do graduates do?What do graduates do?

    They work as game designers, 3D and technical artists, animators, cinematic artists, and interactive content developers. Their paths also lead to game engine programming, AI-based content production, character and IP development, founding independent studios, and graduate study. Recent graduates have joined game companies and research institutes in Korea and abroad, including game direction roles at overseas IT and game companies.

  • Q. Can I go on to graduate school?Can I go on to graduate school?

    Graduates can continue to the Department of Global Creative Arts (interdepartmental program, Master of Arts) and the Department of Applied Art and Technology (M.Eng., Ph.D. in Engineering) at the Graduate School. The Department of Global Creative Arts accepts applicants regardless of their previous department or major, and CAU undergraduate graduates (and expected graduates) are eligible for the CAU GRS scholarship based on academic performance. For details, see Admissions information site ↗.
