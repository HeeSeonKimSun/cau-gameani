2024

Patch Note V3.03rd · Nov 26 – 30, 2024 · Seoul Campus 301 Gallery
11 WORKS

Games use patch notes to document bug fixes and updates, and some games even use them to communicate with their players. Like a game that improves with each patch, our graduates' works were completed after dozens, even hundreds, of rounds of trial and error. Just as a game studio announces its changes through patch notes, this exhibition is where our graduates present their work to visitors through patch notes of their own.

Chung-Ang University Seoul Campus, Art Center 301 Gallery · 9AM–6PM
